0–5 heart scale. Saved player overrides still take priority.

describe moderate flavours and low alcohol tolerance. Hash Brown (+0.5), Sweet
Madame (+0.4) and Harvest's Boon (+0.3) represent balanced meals; Roulette Special
(−0.5), Tandoori Roast Chicken and Rainbow Macarons (−0.3 each) represent alcohol
and excessive spice or sweetness. Parametric Transformer (+1) and Quick Knit
(+0.4) are gameplay nods to research and Scarf.

[Valeriy's profile and voice-overs](https://genshin.aza.gg/character/10000137?l=en)

and describe mineral collecting. Mystique Soup and Glittering Gemstones (+0.5
each), Verdant Gift (+0.4), Strategize (+0.4) and Leave It to Me! (+0.3) reflect
these interests and his work. Roulette Special (−0.5), Fish and Chips and NRE
(−0.3 each) reflect the dislikes. Tactical-card preferences are inferred.

Both entries use `guessed: true`: inventory cards are approximations, not claims
that their exact dishes or gift values are canon. Element and weapon preferences
continue to come from the existing gift system.
