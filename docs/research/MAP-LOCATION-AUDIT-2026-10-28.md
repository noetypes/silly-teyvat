# Location coordinate audit

The catalog contains 117 earlier locations and 42 additions. The new locations copy the coordinates of specific public HoYoLAB map-2 markers directly; their catalog entries say whether each marker is a viewpoint, a pictured point, or a surface projection of an underground scene. The data grid already matches the HoYoLAB interactive map 1:1, so no global coordinate migration was necessary. `python3 tools/map-reference.py --point 5032` retrieves an example marker.

For the 117 earlier entries, the machine-readable [audit](../../work/map-placement-2026-10-28/existing-coordinate-audit.json) records the position, method and any matching anchor or marker. Twenty reproduce a recorded marker within 0.1 map units. Forty-nine are inside a same-name map-label bounding box, which is useful evidence of the general area but does not prove the exact scene. One falls outside same-name label bounds: Apam Woods. Its existing point is close to the named Varunastra viewpoint, and its image is underground. Forty-seven lack an exact-name point or anchor match in this automated check and still need visual or location-specific verification. Do not interpret these counts as proof that all existing waypoints are exact.

This work corrected Lumidouce Harbor, the Fortress of Meropide, Foggy Forest Path, the Fane of Ashvattha, Fort Charybdis and Lumberpick Valley using location-specific HoYoLAB points. The earlier Port Ormos correction is retained. The seed migration changes only the original bundled coordinates; user-moved and deleted pins, custom images, residents and playlists are preserved. Some corrections use an entrance or quest marker near an underground scene; the surface map cannot represent floors. Existing historical scene snapshots retain their original stored data.

The 42 additions and their image, coordinate and music provenance are listed in [LOCATION-CATALOG.md](../guides/LOCATION-CATALOG.md). Some images are low-resolution archive viewpoints or show nearby scenery, and several tracks use a documented containing-area fallback. The 24 new local music files and hashes are documented in [the map music manifest](music/2026-10-29/map-music.json); their binaries remain in `work/map-placement-2026-10-28/music` and the SillyTavern user-file store.
