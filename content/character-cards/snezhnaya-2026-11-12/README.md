# Valeriy and Mitya character cards

Both cards are installed in the local SillyTavern library. For another installation, import either PNG or V2 JSON through SillyTavern Character Management. Each card contains a main greeting, four alternative greetings, example dialogue, a researched portrayal and source notes. Both are playable Electro allies: Mitya uses a catalyst and Valeriy uses a sword. Their gameplay home is Snezhnograd, an existing waypoint; this is not a claim about their private residences. Valeriy’s main greeting is aligned with a visit to the city, while his Armory Palace duties remain in his background.

The PNG display portraits now use the user-supplied Item cards in the standard 208×248 format. Circular portraits use the supplied Icon WebPs, and Burst animations use the supplied Wish WebPs. `supplied-artwork-installation.json` records originals, crops, hashes and metadata preservation. Battle WebPs retain the exact accepted 2026-11-13 TCG illustrations from `art/tcg/2026-11-13-valeriy-mitya-style-revision/trimmed/`. Their approved 22-sprite Original sets are installed in their native character folders. `installation.json` records source paths, hashes, placement and combat adaptations. Original official-portrait cards are backed up in the external pack’s `backups/mitya-valeriy-card-install-2026-11-13/`.

`Valeriy.md` and `Mitya.md` provide readable portrayals and greetings. `sources.json` records the original official artwork URLs, source hashes, fact provenance and research limitations. Direct Wiki profile pages were inaccessible during the original research; the conservative portrayals use indexed material and official introductions. Greetings and dialogue are original fan writing.

The original TCG adaptations in `combat-kits.js` use existing engine effects. Mitya’s Detection Core provides sustained Electro damage, and Gradient Theory adds damage and party attack support. Valeriy’s Bastion Charge shields the party, and Lightning Salvo creates a recurring Electro barrage. Gradient Beacons, Impact Momentum and Stellar-Conduct are approximated rather than implemented as new mechanics. These are not official Genius Invokation kits.
