# Valeriy and Mitya character cards

Both cards are installed in the local SillyTavern library, which is the source of truth; the PNGs here are importable copies of those cards, and the V2 JSON and Markdown files carry the same text. Each card has a main greeting, four alternate greetings and example dialogue (also in `../example-dialogue/`). Both are playable Electro characters: Mitya uses a catalyst and Valeriy uses a sword.

Mitya lives at the Snezhnograd waypoint, the city he escapes to from his research, and his greetings open there. Valeriy lives at Glupov, home of the Armory Palace his battalion guards, and his greetings open there. The card text was rewritten on 2026-11-13 from the wiki's profile, official introductions, character stories and companion dialogue; the greetings and example dialogue are original fan writing.

The PNG display portraits use the user-supplied Item cards in the standard 208×248 format. Circular portraits are face crops of the supplied Icon art (`art/sprites/2026-11-13-valeriy-mitya-face-icons/`), and Burst animations use the supplied Wish art. `supplied-artwork-installation.json` records originals, crops, hashes and metadata preservation. Battle WebPs retain the accepted 2026-11-13 TCG illustrations from `art/tcg/2026-11-13-valeriy-mitya-style-revision/trimmed/`. Their approved 22-sprite sets are installed in their native character folders. `installation.json` records source paths, hashes, placement and combat adaptations. Earlier card versions are backed up in the data pack's `backups/mitya-valeriy-card-install-2026-11-13/` and `backups/mitya-valeriy-card-rewrite-2026-11-13/`.

Their sprites speak English companion lines from the wiki (`docs/research/music/2026-11-13-mitya-valeriy/assignments.json`): the wiki has no Japanese, combat or Elemental Burst voice-overs for them yet, so they have no Burst audio.

The TCG adaptations in `combat-kits.js` use existing engine effects. Mitya's Detection Core provides sustained Electro damage, and Gradient Theory adds damage and party attack support. Valeriy's Bastion Charge shields the party, and Lightning Salvo creates a recurring Electro barrage. Gradient Beacons, Impact Momentum and Stellar-Conduct are approximated rather than implemented as new mechanics. These are not official Genius Invokation kits. Skill, Burst and Normal Attack icons are the wiki's talent icons.
