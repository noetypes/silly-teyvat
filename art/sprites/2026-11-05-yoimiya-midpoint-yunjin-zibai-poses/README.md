# Yoimiya midpoint and dynamic Yun Jin / Zibai poses

Three new neutral candidates were generated independently from the original authorized in-game model images and official portraits. Model images guide design and proportions; official portraits guide illustrated art style only. Previous generated neutrals were not used as generation references.

Yoimiya's prompt aims for proportions between her earlier and especially petite versions. Her comparison shows Earlier neutral, Petite neutral, and New candidate, in that order. Zibai and Yun Jin use more expressive poses while retaining their quiet adeptal and graceful opera-performer characterization respectively. Their comparisons show Previous candidate on the left and New pose on the right.

All three exact new candidates require user approval before expressions. The two Fischl white-heart variants from the previous revision run were approved with the message "Both Fischl good." Their exact hashes and selection records are preserved in the corresponding manifests. Nothing was installed into live SillyTavern.

Candidate PNGs and raw returns are unchanged. Review previews trim transparent margins and scale proportionally; their labels are only on previews. Exact transforms, prompts, reference roles, hashes, and actual tool arguments are recorded in run.json. The built-in tool did not expose or report model, quality or complexity settings.
