# Klee — Blossoming Starlight

All 22 selected sprites are explicitly user-approved.

Both sheets use six columns, read left to right, then top to bottom. No labels are baked into the images.

| Row | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | neutral | annoyance | smug | confusion | love | excitement |
| 2 | joy | fear | pride | desire | anger | grief |
| 3 | sadness | disgust | disappointment | relief | admiration | gratitude |
| 4 | embarrassment | caring | love-1 | desire-1 |  |  |

Each expression was generated independently with built-in image_gen from references/Klee/approved-neutral-v001.png. Smug v003 additionally uses the user-authorized facial-expression reference. Exact prompts and tool arguments are recorded in prompts/Klee/ and run.json.

The love and anticipation expressions depict childlike friendship and eagerness for treats. The -1 variants request white heart-shaped pupils.

Sprites retain their original generated pixels. Full-body sheets uniformly reduce the complete source canvas. Face sheets use the same fixed crop fractions for each image; these are review previews, not new sources.

Technical checks cover filenames, count, PNG decoding, alpha channels, hashes, and reference consistency. No autonomous visual review or correction was performed. The final set is installed as Klee/Blossoming Starlight in SillyTavern and registered as a separate outfit.
