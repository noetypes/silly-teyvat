# Five TCG card retries

New candidates for Aloy, Lohen, Nicole, Odette and Vodyanitsa. The first versions of Sandrone, Zibai, Vesna, Prune and Linnea were explicitly accepted by the user. The accepted artwork remains in the parent `trimmed/` directory. The first and new versions of the five retries are compared in `review.html`.

`generation.json` records the exact prompts and references. `raw/` preserves the generated outputs and `trimmed/` contains the results of `tools/trim-card-corners.py`; its report confirms unchanged RGB and transparent corners.
