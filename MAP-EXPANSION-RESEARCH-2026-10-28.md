# Map expansion research — 28 October 2026

**Historical research shortlist.** Five leads per region (40 total), researched before the catalog update. The installed destinations, final coordinates, artwork and music are documented in [LOCATION-CATALOG.md](LOCATION-CATALOG.md); some labels below were subsequently resolved or refined. A viewpoint is where the observer stands, not necessarily the location of the pictured building.

Kongying Tavern’s [open-source v3 frontend](https://github.com/kongying-tavern/map_front_v3) uses public visitor marker data. Coordinates below are transformed from the named Kongying viewpoint markers using `x = 0.75 × first − 1143`, `y = 0.75 × second + 2941.24`. This empirical conversion was fitted against 219 nearby official viewpoint markers. These coordinates are **approximate**, not a verified point for each depicted interior. The second marker ID is the nearest HoYoLAB viewpoint candidate, not proof of identity. Images from the wiki API are unreviewed candidates.

For implementation, verify marker-to-place and layer; check the background at full size under the asset guide; confirm track conditions, availability, and assignment; then add it. The previously approved shortlist is included. The existing 117 bundled points have not all been audited, and personal waypoints were not inspected.

## Known issues

- Kongying marker 108725 is on Paha Isle, outside the Starsand Shoal anchor; it must not be labelled Starsand Shoal.
- Marker 123116 is the viewpoint of a mansion and may duplicate the existing House of Hesperides. Replace it if the two prove to be the same estate.
- Marker 112320 represents a view toward the Pillar of Embla; the existing Moonlit Pillar Sanctuary is nearby. Establish whether it is a distinct destination before adding it.
- Several lead images are aerial and do not meet the background guide. Ground-level alternatives are recorded in `work/map-expansion-2026-10-28/alternatives.json` and the contact sheets; they have not all been approved.
- Several proposed soundtracks are absent from the existing audio catalog and require media sourcing. Never assign an unrelated regional track as if it were a documented venue theme.
- Mainland Snezhnaya and later map areas have limited independent evidence in the older archived map. The newer Kongying source supplies the marker positions but location and image matching remains to be checked.
